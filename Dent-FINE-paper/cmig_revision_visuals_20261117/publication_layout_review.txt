These are properties of the existing supplied figures; they were not changed.
Do not shrink the new images below full two-column width to imitate the smaller
old schematic text. Full-width placement keeps the new attributions readable.

HOOA plates have identical heights; two-row CMFA and final-Mobile plates have
identical heights. All image panels stay aligned, and no visible title, tick,
