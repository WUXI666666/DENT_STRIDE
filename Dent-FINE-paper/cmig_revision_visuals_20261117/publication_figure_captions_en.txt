New_Prototype
Prototype auxiliary identity supervision on a Mobile example (image 44, reference FDI 51). Independently trained seed-0 models without and with prototype loss predict FDI 11 and 51, respectively. The enabled bank has four prototypes per class and temperature 0.50; prototype inference-score fusion is disabled. Prototype CAM explains the auxiliary class score, not an additional inference classifier. The final panel shows the five highest supported-class LogSumExp scores; these unnormalized scores are not probabilities. Teal boxes show ground truth and orange boxes show predictions. Positive elementwise gradient-times-activation (HiResCAM-form) maps are computed on the full input and normalized by their own full-image maxima before identical cropping. Dashed ground-truth boxes are display references, not attribution masks. Colors represent relative spatial attribution and cannot compare contribution magnitudes between models. Predictions satisfy score >= 0.30 and class-agnostic one-to-one matching at IoU >= 0.50. These selected examples accompany the controlled validation results.

New_FinalMobile
Module-specific attribution in the final Mobile model (EMA seed 2, four prototypes per class, temperature 0.50). Rows show (a) the partly visible reference FDI 51 in image 44 and (b) reference FDI 64 in the blurred image 115. All columns within a row use the same image, checkpoint and native query. Detection, CMFA and HOOA edge CAMs explain the same reference-FDI detection logit at the finest encoder output, finer top-down fusion output and high-pass branch tensor, respectively. Prototype CAM explains the auxiliary prototype class score; prototype scores are not fused at inference. Each positive elementwise gradient-times-activation map is divided by its own full-image maximum before cropping. Orange boxes are predictions and dashed teal boxes are ground truth. The maps show spatial attribution, not comparable contribution magnitudes across modules, and complement rather than replace retrained ablations.

New_FactorSensitivity
Conditional judgment sensitivity of the quadrant, position and dentition heads to input occlusion. Rows show Mobile reference FDI 64, 11 and 46 using the final seed-2 checkpoint (prototype temperature 0.50). Each 1024 by 1024 input is perturbed at 841 locations using a 128 by 128 mean-RGB patch with stride 32. Sensitivity is the unoccluded minus occluded difference in the correct-factor logit margin over its strongest unoccluded alternative; the competitor is fixed. Red indicates support for the correct decision and blue indicates a region whose occlusion increases that margin. Dense maps average the signed margin changes across overlapping patches. Each head shares one symmetric raw logit-margin color scale across all three cases. Encoder top-k anchor indices and query order are fixed to the unoccluded pass; content and downstream predictions are recomputed for every perturbation. Dashed boxes mark the target tooth without masking the sensitivity calculation. These are conditional judgment-sensitivity maps, not native attention maps. Mean-color occlusion may introduce artificial edges.
