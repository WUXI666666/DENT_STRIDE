Two wording refinements recommended before final release:
1. In Discussion principal findings and Limitations, avoid the generic formulation that the reported SDs characterize training variability without limiting the selected-run group. Replace the generic sentences with:
   "Reported standard deviations describe dispersion among the runs included in each summary; the all-five analyses characterize the completed Pano and Mobile runs."
   The current Methods already states this selection limitation accurately; the change removes ambiguity in the standalone Discussion.
2. Scope the Methods sentence "Predictions were retained ... one-to-one matching" to "For the controlled comparison examples, predictions ...". The comparative CAM gallery indeed uses Hungarian matching. In contrast, final_model_cam.py and factor occlusion pick a target-qualified query by IoU and reference-class score rather than all-object Hungarian assignment. The later sentence introduces those final-model analyses separately, so this is precision rather than a numerical defect.

Methods correctly names seed2; the Discussion sentence "The prototype control demonstrates that useful auxiliary supervision can coexist with convergence ..." combines two different-run observations. More exact wording: "The controlled gain and the seed-2 bank diagnostic support auxiliary identity supervision without establishing distinct same-class morphological modes." This need not be inflated into an additional limitation.
