Figure 2. Acquisition-configured DENT-STRIDE architecture. (A) HGNetv2-B0 features at strides 8, 16, and 32 are projected to 256 channels. The highest-stride transformer precedes top-down FPN and bottom-up PAN aggregation. Four fusion sites use CMFA in Mobile and DSLR, and concatenation in Pano; the base fusion blocks restore 256 channels. HOOA refines the three output levels in Mobile. (B) Decoder queries supply box and FDI heads and structured identity supervision. The learned bank contains four prototypes per class (K = 52 in Mobile and 32 in Pano), using temperatures 0.50 and 0.20, respectively. Prototype scores provide training-only auxiliary supervision; factor-logit correction is enabled only in Mobile. The Pano SpatialShape prior modifies candidate logits before sigmoid scoring and top-300 selection. Cohort-specific label maps decode predictions to clinical FDI codes. The DSLR configuration applies additional parameter-rank decay during training, without initialization rollback. Gray paths bypass inactive modules; dashed paths denote auxiliary supervision or training-derived prior estimation. Tensor volumes and prior glyphs are schematic.
