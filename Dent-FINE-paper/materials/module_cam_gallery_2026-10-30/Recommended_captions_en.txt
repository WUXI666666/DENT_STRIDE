Recommended_CMFA_HiResCAM
Query-specific visualization of CMFA on DSLR validation images. Columns show reference annotations, control CAM, CMFA CAM, and predictions from separately trained models without and with CMFA. The paired runs use AdamW and seed 0. The examples correct FDI 55 to 16, 32 to 72, and 81 to 41, respectively. Teal boxes indicate ground truth and orange boxes indicate predictions. CAMs explain the reference-FDI logit at the finest encoder output using positive elementwise gradient-times-activation. Each map is normalized by its full-image maximum before identical cropping; colors show relative spatial attribution, not comparable magnitudes across models. Predictions use score≥0.30 and one-to-one localization matching at IoU≥0.50. The selected cases complement the controlled quantitative evaluation.

Recommended_HOOA_HiResCAM
Effect of the high-pass and second-order mechanisms in HOOA on a Mobile validation example. The reduced model replaces the second-order interaction with the context branch and removes the high-pass contribution; other HOOA branches remain active. Both models are independently trained at seed 0 with prototype temperature 0.20. Complete HOOA corrects a primary FDI 53 prediction to the reference permanent FDI 13. CAMs target the same reference-FDI class at the independently matched native query in each model. Teal boxes denote ground truth and orange boxes denote predictions. Maps use positive elementwise gradient-times-activation with per-map full-image maximum normalization. This is an illustrative correction rather than a correction-frequency estimate.

Recommended_Prototype_mechanism
Prototype auxiliary supervision and FDI identification on Mobile validation images. Separately trained seed-0 models without prototype supervision and with four prototypes per class at temperature 0.50 are compared. The examples correct FDI 11 to 51, 63 to 62, and 83 to 42. Prototype CAMs explain the actual auxiliary class score using positive elementwise gradient-times-activation; they do not explain an additional inference classification path. Prototype score fusion at inference is disabled. The final column shows the five highest auxiliary scores among classes with training support, computed by LogSumExp aggregation of temperature-scaled query–prototype cosine similarities; the reference class is teal. Scores are unnormalized and are not probabilities. These examples illustrate identity supervision, not specialization of the four prototype slots.

Recommended_FinalMobile_modules_HiResCAM
Module-specific spatial attributions in the final DENT-STRIDE-Mobile model. All panels within a row use the same image, trained EMA checkpoint (seed 2, four prototypes per class, temperature 0.50), and native query. Detection CAM, CMFA CAM, and HOOA edge CAM explain the same reference-FDI detection logit at the finest encoder output, finer top-down CMFA output, and high-pass branch tensor, respectively. Prototype CAM explains the auxiliary prototype class score. Positive elementwise gradient-times-activation is normalized separately by each full-image maximum, so colors do not compare contribution strength across modules. Orange boxes are predictions and dashed teal boxes are ground truth. These mechanism illustrations complement, but do not replace, independently retrained module ablations.

CAM interpretation: gradients do not traverse the native detached query-content initialization and therefore describe the remaining differentiable paths. Bilinear interpolation is for display only. CAMs are not native attention values, segmentation masks, probabilities, or proof of causal or clinical effectiveness. No target mask is used to calculate the attribution. The same limitations apply to all four figures above.
